# Map pass 2 — the swarm's findings, blast radius on the Map (proposal, 2026-10-03)

**Status:** agreed 2026-10-03 from the eight-persona swarm on the Map (reports outside the repo); implementation in
lanes Z · N · L · K (wave 1) and I (wave 2). Companion: `dependencies-and-nx.md` (lanes D · X · C).

## 1. What the swarm said

Eight personas drove the Map read-only on a real application (18 journeys) and judged from their own screenshots:
8 × TRIAL, fit 6.25; *understand the application* Map 6.8 · Journeys 6.75 · Portfolio 6.3 · Code map 3.9; **six of
eight would open the Map first** (the question that scored 1.9 in the September story swarm). Every role credited
the screen → call → table chain and the one-screen card. What they converged on, in order:

1. **One journey, three screen counts** (8/8): the card says 14, the property footer 10, Drill 23. They are *declared*
   screens, screens the walk *reached*, and action *stops*; nothing says so.
2. **The opening zoom and Fit** (8/8): a journey opens at ×0.52 off-centre with half its screens off canvas; Fit and
   `−` throw you out to the whole board and drop the journey from the crumb.
3. **Zoom changes mode uninvited** (5/8): a few wheel ticks skip the calls level and snap into a screen nobody chose.
   The owner's own experience on a trackpad is the opposite: *"very intuitive to zoom in and out cleanly."* The two
   are compatible: a mouse wheel notch is `deltaY ≈ 100`, a trackpad step is a few pixels, and automation sends
   notches. The engine must be intuitive on every input, not only the one it was built on.
4. **No blast radius on the Map** (7/8): "how far does a change reach" sends everyone to the Code map's IMPACT.
5. Truncated titles, chips and names; the canvas under the toolbar (7/8). Unlabelled lines, no legend (7/8).
   Tooltips covering what they describe (6/8). Test counts without their evidence class (5/8). The business lens
   stopping short of the data nodes (5/8). Owner and ERP-reach absent (5/8). "Map" meaning three things (5/8).
   Changes tab showing index facts, not the application's commits (3/8). Keyboard reaching one journey of eighteen;
   zoom and pan not in the link; the theme icon opening full Settings (single voices).

## 2. Decisions

- **Zoom-to-enter stays.** It is "the moment" for two personas and the owner; it becomes *earned*: the wheel and `+`
  stop at the readable calls level first, and the snap fires only when one screen is centred and large.
- **One number names its scope, everywhere it appears.** `14 screens declared · 10 reached by the walk`; the footer
  `screen 4 of 10 reached`; Drill keeps *stops* and says so. No count without its unit.
- **Blast radius is a mode of the Map, not a tab.** Pick a thing (a screen, a call, a table, a dependency, a work
  item, a commit) and the whole board dims to what it affects, at every altitude; the property gains an *Affected*
  tab. Dependencies (lane D) feed the same mode once they exist.
- The registers finish the job: hybrid gets file:line and identifiers, business gets words.

## 4. Lane I — blast radius on the Map (wave 2, after Z and N)

`feat/map-affected`: an **Affected** mode. Entry points: the explore card's *What's affected* action on a screen, call,
table or dependency; a work item or commit picked from the property's Work / Changes tabs; `?affected=<nodeId|work:KEY|
commit:sha>` in the link. The mode asks `/api/impact?node=&direction=upstream&flows=1&tests=1` (per hop, never summed)
— for a work item or commit, the seeds are its touched parts — and draws: on the board, districts dimmed unless a
journey is reached, with the hop at which it is reached; on the street, screens and calls dimmed unless reached, the
seed ringed; in the property, an *Affected* tab: journeys · screens · calls · tests that reach it, per hop, with the
*≥ a floor* word and the cut points the impact report carries. A **dependencies** seed (lane D) lights every journey a
third-party or workspace package reaches. The *also in* row and the data card's *on* list are folded into this mode's
words. Numbers: the impact report's own `Counted`s; nothing new is summed.
